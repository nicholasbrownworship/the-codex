Philosophy

How Music Gets Made

🌱

Organic First

Every idea starts unplugged. Piano and guitar are the sketchbook — low friction, immediate feedback, no menus to navigate.

🔁

Exploratory

The process is loose by design. Many ideas get tried. Most get discarded. The ones that survive earn their spot in the DAW.

🎼

Cinematic Ambition

The brass background gives orchestral writing a natural home. The dream is music that makes a D&D table feel like they're inside a film score.

✏️

Songwriter's Mind

Lyrics and melody come from the same instinct as storytelling — both are trying to make someone feel something specific at a specific moment.
